In local shops, creating awareness about digitalized money transactions is crucial for embracing 
modern payment methods. This involves educating both shop owners and customers about the 
benefits and ease of using digital payment options such as mobile wallets, QR code payments, or 
card transactions. Shop owners can be encouraged to display signs promoting digital payment 
acceptance and offer incentives like discounts or loyalty rewards for digital transactions. 
Simultaneously, customers can be informed about the convenience and security of digital payments,helping them feel more confident in making the switch from cash. Overall, fostering awareness and 
trust in digital transactions at local shops can contribute to a smoother transition towards a cashless 
economy.
